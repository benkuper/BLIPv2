# 5.1 Battery sensing and state

The battery model is implemented in `v2/components/blip_power`. It
converts a calibrated ADC-pin reading through a board divider ratio, filters the
battery voltage with a bounded integer IIR, estimates a voltage-based charge
percentage, and uses separate entry and recovery thresholds for low battery.
Invalid calibration and readings leave the last good state intact. The
`blip_power_tests` host test covers the known HUZZAH32 2:1 divider, percentage
limits, filtering, hysteresis, and invalid readings.

The HUZZAH32 ESP-IDF component uses ADC1 on GPIO35 with line fitting
calibration and a four-reading average. The registry exposes `pin_mv`,
`battery_mv`, `filtered_mv`, `soc_per_mille`, `low`, `valid`,
`sample_errors`, and `divider_numerator` under `blip.power.battery`.
The board build opts in with `BLIP_BOARD_ADAFRUIT_HUZZAH32=ON`. A serial HIL
reading is recorded in
`docs/v2/evidence/board-bringup/2026-10-01-huzzah32-battery.json`.

The HUZZAH32 divider ratio comes from the [Adafruit power management
guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-huzzah32-esp32-feather/power-management).
The voltage endpoints and low threshold are initial policy values, not a
measured state-of-charge curve. A voltage estimate under LED and radio load is
not a fuel gauge.

This work package remains open. Divider values for Creators Ball V2 and Creators
Tab need board-specific electrical calibration before those boards report
battery voltage or percentage. M5StickC and M5Dial require their power hardware
to be identified before a battery source is declared. The XIAO C6 profile has
no onboard battery monitor in its current manifest.
