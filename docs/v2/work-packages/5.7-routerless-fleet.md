# 5.7 Routerless ESP-NOW fleet

Fleet is intended for props operating without a router or computer. The initial
LAN integration was replaced during development following the user's requirement.
ESP-NOW broadcast removes the protocol's paired-device limit from fan-out. Silent
followers need no participant roster or routine ACK traffic. Memory and steady
transmit work are fixed per prop; physical radio capacity is still finite.

The [component specification](../../../v2/components/blip_fleet/README.md)
describes election, one-way beacon clock estimates, bounded repeated cues,
duplicate windows, radio leases and registry controls. Leader loss cancels queued
work and starts a jittered election. Returning leaders relock follower clocks.
Direct computer control and cue execution run independently of the radio worker.

`BLIP_ENABLE_FLEET=ON` includes this optional component. Fleet settings default
to disabled, ID 1, channel 1. Active mode temporarily pauses association/scanning
and setup AP advertising on the prop, and runs ESP-NOW on the chosen channel. Disable
restores saved networking and power-save policy. The shared ESP-NOW owner keeps
broadcast fleet and unicast peer control from overwriting the SDK callback or
deinitializing a radio still in use.

## Evidence

All 21 host executables pass. Broadcast engine cases include 4,096 simulated
silent listeners, constant broadcast work, different uptimes, cue loss/retries,
reordering, duplicates, leader loss/reboot, partition merging, stale samples and
queue/deadline limits. The listener simulation is not a physical scale claim.

The [routerless Ball/HUZZAH32 pair gate](../evidence/fleet/2026-10-01-routerless-pair.json)
passed on 2026-10-01. Both props reported autonomous channel 1, autonomous state 6,
and no setup AP advertising, without router association. Four integer cues and a reset action executed on both
nodes while direct serial writes remained usable. After withdrawing the Ball,
the HUZZAH32 took over; observed recovery, including serial polling overhead,
was 2.70 seconds. Its replacement-leader cue also executed. The Ball rejoined,
was elected again, and both clocks relocked. The abandoned cue stayed cancelled.

The Ball executed five commands and the HUZZAH32 six, with zero execution failures,
executor drops or late cue drops. The follower suppressed 81 duplicate cue copies.
Reported maximum software dispatch lateness was 6,569 us on the Ball and 3,742 us
on the HUZZAH32. This measures software dispatch against estimated fleet time,
not physical LED alignment. Fleet/probe settings and both radio leases were
restored and verified. The PC remained on its ordinary network.

The [seven-board build/flash gate](../evidence/fleet/2026-10-01-seven-board-build.json)
passed for Ball, HUZZAH32, XIAO C6, M5Dial, M5StickC, Creators Tab and Creators Club.
Every image fits its app partition, includes fleet and unicast peer control, and
has a verified flash write. The Ball also includes NimBLE.

The [final seven-board group gate](../evidence/fleet/2026-10-01-seven-board-group.json)
passed with setup AP advertising suppressed on every prop. All nodes elected
the Ball and locked their clocks. An integer cue executed once on every node
alongside a direct serial write. A future cue was queued everywhere, then leader
withdrawal cancelled it. The XIAO was elected by the remaining six nodes and
executed another cue across that group. The Ball rejoined and all clocks relocked.
Complete replacement consensus was observed after 19.84 seconds, including two
sequential serial polling passes; this is not a precise device failover measurement.

There were zero execution failures, executor drops and late cue drops across the
seven boards. Maximum reported software lateness was 9,278 us. Settings and radio
leases were restored and verified on every node. The computer stayed connected
to `Archi-wifi guest 2` with Internet access.

The [peer-control resumption gate](../evidence/fleet/2026-10-01-peer-resume.json)
also passed: unicast reads worked before and after a temporary autonomous fleet
lease, with peer and fleet settings restored. The serial HIL helpers are under
`v2/tools/control/` and can repeat the pair, group and resumption checks.

The Plan 5.7 functional recovery gate passed. Remaining qualification: physical
multi-node timing, high-interference/startup tests, longer soak and authenticated
broadcast. The present transport is single hop; relay routing is not implemented.
Broadcast repetition is best effort and does not prove every listener received or
executed a cue. The one-way clock reports an assumed 5 ms link budget rather than
a measured precision guarantee.
