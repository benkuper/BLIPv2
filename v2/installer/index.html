BLIP V2 · factory installer

Flash. Connect. Create.

This package writes a complete factory image, including the initialized browser-control filesystem. Confirm that the package matches your board before flashing: chip detection alone cannot identify the board model or its flash capacity.

  1. After flashing, join the open BLIP-xxxxxx Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1 and use the generated Wi-Fi provisioning action.
  3. Reconnect to your normal network, open the device, and use Firmware update for later A/B releases.
